Mock Interview

Draw a problem, start the clock, and talk it through out loud: clarify the job and whether it needs several agents at all, then the agents and their briefs, then how they coordinate, then what can fail, then how you would know the team beats one agent. Concept questions and design questions use the same phases. The timer tells you what you should be doing; the rubric afterwards tells you what you skipped. Scoring yourself honestly is where the improvement comes from.

    How to practise so it actually transfers

    Speak out loud — silent thinking is the most common thing that separates people who know the material from people who pass interviews. Draw the diagram. When the timer moves to a new phase, move on even if you're not finished; running out of time in the deep-dive phase is the classic failure. Afterwards, look up the one thing you were least sure about and read that chapter section again.
