    How to practise so it actually transfers

    Speak out loud — silent thinking is the most common thing that separates people who know the material from people who pass interviews. Write the SQL, not just the idea of it. When the timer moves to a new phase, move on even if you're not finished; never reaching indexes and concurrency is the classic failure. Afterwards, look up the one thing you were least sure about and read that chapter section again.
