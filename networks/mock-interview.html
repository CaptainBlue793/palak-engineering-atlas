    How to practise so it actually transfers

    Speak out loud — silent thinking is the most common thing that separates people who know the material from people who pass interviews. Draw the path. When the timer moves to a new phase, move on even if you're not finished; running out of time in the deep-dive phase is the classic failure. Afterwards, look up the one thing you were least sure about and read that chapter section again.
