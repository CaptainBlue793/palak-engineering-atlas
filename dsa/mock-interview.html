    How to practise so it actually transfers

    Solve it out loud — silent thinking is the single most common thing separating people who know the material from people who pass interviews. State the brute force before you optimise, even when the optimal answer is obvious to you; an interviewer cannot give you credit for a step you skipped in your head. When the timer moves on, move on. And always spend the last five minutes dry-running your own code on the smallest failing case you can think of — finding your own bug is worth more than never having written it.
