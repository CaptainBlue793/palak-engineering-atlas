    How to practise so it actually transfers

    Speak out loud and draw the messages, not just the boxes. Interviewers in this round push on one thing above all: “and what if this node dies right here?” Practise answering that for every arrow in your diagram. When the timer moves on, move on; running out of time before the failure walkthrough is the classic way to lose this round. Afterwards, reread the chapter linked to the problem.
